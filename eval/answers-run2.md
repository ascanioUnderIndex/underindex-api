## 1. Goth Ninja: from memory

~~~text
The Goth Ninja Outfit

When it was big: “Goth Ninja” took off on Reddit fashion boards, Tumblr, and early Grailed around 2011–2012 and peaked from about 2013 to 2016. It then gave way to logo-heavy hypebeast streetwear and later to techwear. Since around 2021 it has come back under the names “darkwear” and the “Opium” look, helped by Playboi Carti’s circle and a broad Rick Owens revival. So it’s a 2010s aesthetic with a real current afterlife.

The silhouette: It’s all black (with the occasional grey or bone), long on top and narrow or drop-crotch on the bottom. Layers are drapey and often asymmetric, and the shoes are chunky.

The key pieces

Base layer: long, drapey tee or tank

Grail: Rick Owens Level Tee or a DRKSHDW long tee. The long, slightly sheer hem is the signature.
Budget: COS or Uniqlo oversized tees. Buy a size up and let them hang.

Mid layer: cowl-neck hoodie or long cardigan

Grail: Julius gasmask hoodie, the most iconic Goth Ninja piece of all. Alternatives are a Boris Bidjan Saberi (11 by BBS) hoodie or an Ann Demeulemeester long cardigan.
Budget: Zara or H&M funnel-neck or asymmetric zip hoodies, which came and went in waves all through the 2010s.

Outer layer: leather jacket or long coat

Grail: Rick Owens Stooges or Intarsia leather jacket, or an Isaac Sellam jacket if you want rougher, waxed leather.
Mid: Helmut Lang or Alexander Wang leather, which are easy to find secondhand.
Budget: Secondhand AllSaints leather, a reliable entry point.

Bottoms: drop-crotch or tapered cargo pants

Grail: Julius gasmask cargos, Rick Owens Drawstring Cargo or DRKSHDW Berlin/Prisoner pants, or 11 by BBS.
Budget: Drop-crotch joggers from Zara, H&M, or ASOS, or slim black cargos tapered by a tailor.

Footwear: the deciding factor

Grail: Rick Owens Geobaskets or Ramones, Guidi 988 back-zip boots, or Carol Christian Poell if you’re truly committed.
Mid: Y-3 Qasa High. It defined the 2013–2015 era and is widely available used.
Budget: All-black Nike Roshe Runs, Converse Chuck 70 Hi in black, or generic side-zip combat boots.

Accessories: These are optional but help. Think a long black scarf or snood, silver jewelry (Werkstatt:München at the grail end), and a black leather tote or crossbody. Keep it minimal.

Where to buy

New: SSENSE, END., Mr Porter, Antonioli, Dover Street Market, and Rick Owens’ own boutiques.

Secondhand: Grailed is the home of this aesthetic and the best place for used Rick, Julius, BBS, and Guidi. Vestiaire Collective and eBay also work. Budget: COS, Uniqlo, Zara, H&M, and ASOS.

Realistic budgets
Tier	Approach	Rough total
Entry	High-street basics plus used Y-3 Qasas	€300–600
Mid	DRKSHDW pieces, used Rick or Julius from Grailed, used Geobaskets	€1,500–3,000
Grail	Full Rick mainline or Julius at retail, Guidi boots, leather jacket	€6,000–10,000+

The leather jacket is usually the most expensive piece, around €2,500–3,500 retail for Rick mainline. Next are the shoes: Geobaskets and Guidis each run about €1,000–1,300 new.

These prices are from my own knowledge, not a live check, and designer prices have risen steadily, so expect current retail to be at or above these figures.

Practical tip: If you’re spending money, put it into the shoes and the pants first. Those two pieces carry the silhouette, and cheap black layers on top read as Goth Ninja surprisingly well.
~~~

## 1. Goth Ninja: with the record

~~~text
Goth Ninja Outfit (circa 2009–2014)

Goth Ninja is the dark, drapey, layered look that took shape on menswear forums like Styleforum and Superfuture in the late 2000s and peaked in the early 2010s. Your record’s 2009–2014 window fits. The core idea is lots of thin black and grey layers, odd proportions (long on top, low crotch below), and heavy artisanal footwear. It’s not modern techwear, so skip the straps, buckles, Gore-Tex, and tactical cargo pockets. The silhouette should look soft and draped, not armored.

The outfit, top to bottom
Base layer: extra-long draped tee. A Rick Owens or DRKSHDW long tee in black or “dust” grey, hanging to mid-thigh, ideally with a slightly asymmetric hem. Thin, slubby jersey matters more than the logo. Expect about $80–200 used.
Mid layer: hooded cardigan or wrap top. Julius (Tatsuro Horikawa’s label) is the signature here: a thin, long hooded knit or jersey cardigan, often with a deep hood and a cowl front. Damir Doma wrap cardigans are a good alternative. Expect $150–400 used.
Optional outer layer: draped jacket or long vest. A Rick Owens or Ann Demeulemeester draped-collar jacket, or a long sleeveless jersey vest, adds a third layer. Layering three or four thin pieces is what makes it read as Goth Ninja rather than just “all black.”
Bottoms: drop-crotch shorts over leggings. Rick Owens or DRKSHDW “pods”-style shorts with a deep drop crotch, worn over Julius or Rick leggings. If that’s too much for you, Damir Doma or Rick drop-crotch trousers tapered at the ankle are the safer version. Expect $150–400 for the shorts or trousers and $80–200 for leggings.
Footwear: Geobaskets. Rick Owens Geobaskets are the defining shoe of the look: chunky, high-top, and oversized. Alternatives are Rick Owens Dunks, or Guidi back-zip or horse-leather boots for a more artisanal, less sneaker-y feel. Geobaskets usually run about $400–900 used, depending on colorway and condition.
Accessories. A long black jersey scarf or snood, a leather wrap bracelet or simple silver, and maybe a soft, unstructured leather bag. Keep it minimal.

For a more avant-garde or deep-cut version, look at 11 by Boris Bidjan Saberi (11 by BBS) and Boris Bidjan Saberi mainline, which add a slightly more rugged, dyed-and-washed texture. Carol Christian Poell is the ultimate grail brand of the era, though it’s very expensive and rare.

Where to buy

Grailed is the easiest starting point, since this aesthetic’s whole archive circulates there and you can search by designer and era. Yahoo Auctions Japan is excellent for Julius and Japanese-market Rick, often at better prices. You’ll need a proxy service such as Buyee or ZenMarket. Outside Japan, factor in proxy fees, international shipping, and import duties, which can add a lot to the sticker price. eBay and Vestiaire Collective are also worth checking. For new pieces, Rick Owens and DRKSHDW are still sold through the brand’s own stores and major luxury retailers, though current collections have moved away from the 2010–2013 cuts.

Realistic budget

Your record’s $600–$2,500 range holds up for buying secondhand, with some caveats. The low end, around $600–900, is doable only with DRKSHDW tees and shorts, a used Julius piece, and a cheaper shoe like DRKSHDW Ramones instead of Geobaskets. A solid, faithful outfit with real Geobaskets, Julius layers, and Rick or Damir Doma bottoms realistically lands around $1,000–2,000 used. Buying comparable pieces new at retail can easily exceed $3,000–5,000, mostly because of the footwear and outerwear. Resale prices move with trends, so treat these as rough estimates rather than current quotes.
~~~

## 2. Acubi Style: from memory

~~~text
Acubi Style Outfit

Acubi is a Korean-born look built on a muted palette (grey, black, washed blue, sage, off-white), layering, slim or shrunken tops over looser bottoms, and a slightly grungy, sporty edge. The name comes from the Korean label Acubi Club, whose aesthetic became shorthand for the whole style.

Which years it belongs to

It grew out of Seoul street style around 2021–2022, went viral on TikTok and Pinterest in 2023, and peaked through 2023–2024. It’s very much an early-2020s aesthetic, tied to that era’s Y2K revival and Korean fashion boom. I haven’t checked how it’s trending in late 2026, so treat that as background rather than a forecast.

The outfit

Base layer: a ruched or mesh long-sleeve top in grey or black. Acubi Club and Rest & Recreation do the authentic versions. For cheaper options, look at Weekday, Pull&Bear, or Bershka.

Over it: a shrunken cropped zip-up hoodie or cropped knit cardigan in heather grey. Matin Kim is the classic Korean pick for knits. Uniqlo, COS, and Brandy Melville do convincing budget equivalents.

Bottoms: low-rise wide-leg cargo or parachute pants in washed grey or faded black. An alternative is an asymmetric or layered mini skirt over leggings. Andersson Bell and Marithé + François Girbaud (huge in Korea) are the brand-name route. Zara, Weekday, and Urban Outfitters cover the cheap end.

Shoes: chunky retro runners or platform Mary Janes. For runners, the New Balance 530 or Asics Gel-1130 are the signature choices in silver, grey, or white. Mary Janes can be worn with ribbed leg warmers scrunched over them.

Accessories: a small nylon shoulder bag (Matin Kim’s are iconic), layered silver jewelry, a double belt slung low, and a claw clip or messy half-up hair.

Where to buy

For authentic Korean brands, Musinsa Global is the main platform, and W Concept and YesStyle also ship internationally. Many brands (Matin Kim, Acubi Club, Andersson Bell) also sell through their own websites. Shipping from Korea can add EU import VAT and customs fees, so factor that in. For high-street alternatives, Zara, Weekday, COS, Pull&Bear, Bershka, Uniqlo, and Urban Outfitters all carry pieces that fit, especially in their grey and washed-denim ranges. Vinyl or secondhand apps like Vinted are also great for zip-ups and cargos.

Realistic budget (approximate, in euros)
Piece	High street	Korean brands
Ruched/mesh top	€15–30	€50–80
Cropped zip-up or cardigan	€25–45	€70–130
Cargo pants or skirt	€30–50	€90–180
Sneakers	€90–130	€110–150
Bag, belt, jewelry, leg warmers	€30–50	€100–200
Total	~€190–300	~€420–740

These prices come from my general knowledge rather than current listings, so check before buying. A smart middle route is to spend on the sneakers and one hero Korean piece (like a Matin Kim knit or bag) and get the rest on the high street.
~~~

~~~text
The Opium Look: A Full Outfit

Opium is the aesthetic tied to Playboi Carti’s Opium label and its artists, Ken Carson and Destroy Lonely. It runs from 2019 to the present. It took off around the Whole Lotta Red era (2020–21) and has stayed a defining look in rage-rap circles since. The rules are to keep it almost entirely black, make the proportions dramatic and distorted, and use plenty of leather. A plain goth outfit with lace, velvet, or Victorian details is not Opium. What matters here is the avant-garde silhouette and the heavy materials.

The Outfit

Outerwear: leather jacket or trench coat. This is the centerpiece. A Rick Owens leather jacket, such as a cropped biker or a longer leather coat, gives you the sharp, distorted shape the look depends on. A floor-grazing black leather trench is the other classic choice if you want more drama.

Top: oversized, long-line tee or hoodie. Use a Rick Owens DRKSHDW elongated tee that hangs well past the hip, or a boxy, distressed Balenciaga hoodie. It should look stretched and off-proportion, not neat.

Bottoms: distressed black denim. Rick Owens DRKSHDW denim is the standard pick. Choose a flared or stacked cut that pools over your boots, faded or distressed black.

Footwear: chunky leather boots. Rick Owens boots are the strongest choice for the look, including the high-top Geobasket sneakers. Balenciaga’s heavy combat-style boots also work. You want weight and bulk at the bottom.

Accessories: hardware and silver. A 1017 ALYX 9SM rollercoaster-buckle belt adds the industrial hardware detail. Chunky silver rings and chains finish the look, and Chrome Hearts is the go-to brand in this scene. Givenchy pieces from the Matthew Williams era (roughly 2020–2023) fit too, since they share Alyx’s hardware-heavy style.

Where to Buy

For new pieces, use SSENSE and Rick Owens boutiques. SSENSE carries Rick Owens, DRKSHDW, Balenciaga, and Alyx, and its seasonal sales can cut prices a lot. For resale, use Grailed, which is where much of this community actually shops, along with Vestiaire Collective and The RealReal. Chrome Hearts is mostly sold through its own stores, so resale is often the easier route for that brand. Authenticate expensive pieces carefully on resale platforms, because Rick Owens and Chrome Hearts are often faked.
~~~

## 4. Ura-Hara (Harajuku 90s): from memory

~~~text
Ura-Hara Outfit Guide
The era

Ura-Harajuku (“back-street Harajuku”) was the streetwear scene in the side alleys behind Omotesandō in Tokyo. It began in the early 1990s with Hiroshi Fujiwara’s Goodenough (around 1990). It took shape when Nigo and Jun Takahashi opened the shop Nowhere in 1993, the same year A Bathing Ape launched. The peak was roughly 1995–1999, driven by magazines like Boon, Popeye and Smart, tiny print runs, and queues outside unmarked shops. By the early-to-mid 2000s the scene had gone mainstream and spread out. So 1993–2002 is the core window, and 1996–1999 is the classic look.

The outfit

Cap: A Goodenough logo cap or a BAPE ape head cap. Goodenough is the deeper cut, since Fujiwara is the godfather of the whole scene.

Tee: An oversized ape head tee from A Bathing Ape, or a Goodenough graphic tee. Wear it a size or two up so it hangs long.
~~~
