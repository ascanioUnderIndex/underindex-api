## 1. Goth Ninja: record with planted errors

~~~text
Goth Ninja Outfit

One correction first. The record’s 1999–2004 date range is wrong, and it contradicts the record’s own details. Goth Ninja took shape around 2010–2011 on menswear forums like Styleforum and Superfuture, peaked roughly 2012–2016, and has stayed alive as a niche since. The rest of the record points the same way: it labels Rick Owens “early 2010s,” Death Grips formed in 2010, witch house emerged around 2009–2010, and Dark Souls came out in 2011.

The outfit
Top layer: Julius hooded cardigan in black or charcoal, with an asymmetric zip or open drape. Julius is the most “ninja” of the brands. Damir Doma is a softer, more earthy alternative.

Grailed is the main source for secondhand Rick, Julius, and 11 by BBS. ## 4. Ura-Hara (Harajuku 90s): record with planted errors
~~~

~~~text
The reference record gets the era slightly wrong and includes a few brands that don’t belong. I’ve built the outfit around what Ura-Hara actually was and flagged the corrections at the end.

The era

Ura-Hara (“back-streets Harajuku”) peaked from roughly 1993 to 1999. Its roots go back to the late ’80s, when Hiroshi Fujiwara was DJing, co-founding the Major Force hip-hop label, and bringing back New York and London street culture. The defining moment came in 1993, when NIGO and Jun Takahashi opened the Nowhere shop and A Bathing Ape and Undercover started from it. The scene faded in the early 2000s as Bape went global and the original small-run, hard-to-get model disappeared.

The outfit
Tee: an early A Bathing Ape or Goodenough graphic tee. Look for a heavyweight cotton tee with a big ape-head or early camo print, or a Goodenough logo tee from Fujiwara’s 1990 label. Limited drops and friends-only sales defined the era, so the tee carries the outfit.
Jacket: a wool varsity jacket with leather sleeves. Early Bape varsities are ideal, but an unbranded ’90s Japanese varsity also fits. If you want something more punk, swap in an early Undercover piece, though anything from the iconic seasons like AW97 “Scab” is grail-priced.
Denim: relaxed-fit selvedge jeans. Evisu (founded in Osaka in 1991, with the painted seagull on the back pocket) is the most period-correct choice. A pair of vintage Levi’s 501s worn baggy is the cheaper route.
Sneakers: Nike Air Max 95s or Air Force 1s. Japan’s mid-’90s “Air Max hunting” craze is pure Ura-Hara. Avoid Bape Stas, since they came out in 2002, after the peak.
Accessories: a Head Porter or Porter nylon bag, plus a camo bucket hat or logo cap from Bape or Goodenough. Small accessories like these were how many kids got into the scene at entry-level prices.
Where to buy

Yahoo Auctions Japan and Mercari Japan have the deepest stock of original pieces. You’ll usually need a proxy service such as Buyee, ZenMarket, or FromJapan to buy from them. Grailed is easier to use but pricier. Japanese secondhand chains like 2nd Street turn up good finds, and if you’re ever in Tokyo, the Harajuku and Shimokitazawa vintage shops are where this stuff still circulates.

Budget

These are ballpark figures from memory, and vintage prices swing a lot with condition and rarity:

Bape or Goodenough tee: about $100–400
Varsity jacket: about $300–800 for vintage Bape, less for unbranded
Evisu or vintage 501s: about $100–300
Air Max 95 or AF1 (new retro): about $120–200
Bag and hat: about $50–200

Total: roughly $700–1,900. A thrift-and-unbranded build lands near $400, and early Undercover pieces can push a single item past $2,000.

Corrections to the record
Yeezy doesn’t belong. It’s Kanye West’s 2010s brand and has nothing to do with ’90s Harajuku.
Number (N)ine and WTAPS both started in 1996. WTAPS is part of the extended Ura-Hara family, along with Neighborhood (1994). Number (N)ine is better described as a later Japanese designer label than a core Ura-Hara brand.
“Mid 80s – Early 90s” is a few years too early. The scene peaked in the mid-to-late ’90s.
Jet Set Radio (2000) and Shenmue (1999) fit the aesthetic as cultural cousins rather than direct parts of the movement. The Beastie Boys connection is real, through their friendships with Fujiwara and NIGO.
~~~

~~~text
Atticus is a stretch. Blink-182 members started it in 2001, and it belongs much more to pop-punk and the later scene look than to Mall Goth, so I’d skip it for this outfit. The other four brands (Tripp NYC, Lip Service, JNCO, and Demonia) are solid choices.
~~~
