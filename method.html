Method

How the records are made.

What a record contains, what "verified" means here, how errors get fixed, and what is still missing.

Who makes them

UnderIndex is built by Ascanio, an independent developer in Italy. Records are researched by hand. If you find an error, open an issue on GitHub.

What's in a record

FieldWhat it holds
YearsThe period the style belongs to
DescriptionOne line on what the style is
KeyBrandsA short list of the brands that define it
IconicItemsThe pieces it is recognised by
MusicGenre, Artists, VideoGamesThe culture around it
WhereToBuyShops and resale sites where it turns up
PriceRangeA rough budget for the look
NotesA WARNING on where the style ends and what it must not be confused with

The WARNING is there because the usual error isn't an invented brand. It's a real brand from the style next door, so the answer still sounds right.

What "verified" means here

It means a person researched the record by hand and that it can be challenged and corrected in public. It doesn't mean more than that:

  • No third party has certified the data.
  • Sources aren't published field by field yet, so you can't trace a single brand back to a citation.
  • 15 of the 60 records have been through the public review described below. The other 45 haven't.

How errors get found and fixed

In the public eval, Claude answered outfit questions on 15 subcultures with the record in context, and it questioned 9 of the 15 records. Each point was checked against web sources, and the 9 records were corrected on 14 October 2026 (dataset version 9ab6258f9c51):

  • Acubi Style adds Acubi Club and moves to $150 - $800.
  • Opium moves to $800 - $5000.
  • Ura-Hara starts in 1993, replaces Number (N)ine with Goodenough and Neighborhood, drops Shenmue and moves to $400 - $2000.
  • Health Goth credits the 2013 Portland Facebook page and drops Vaporwave.
  • Indie Sleaze starts in 2006 and swaps The RealReal for eBay.
  • Whimsigoth drops American McGee's Alice.
  • Frutiger Aero swaps Mirror's Edge for Wii Sports.
  • Terrace Casuals swaps The Jam for The Farm.
  • Gyaru labels Liz Lisa as Hime Gyaru and MA*RS as Agejo.

A model is a useful reviewer but not a sufficient one. When errors were planted in records on purpose, Claude called out 13 of 15 wrong dates and 10 of 15 brands from neighbouring scenes, but plausible wrong brands still reached the outfit in 4 of 15 answers. The data has to be right at the source.

Versions

Every response carries a dataset_version, a hash of the published records. It changes whenever a record changes, so you can cache safely and see when something moved.

Limits

  • Boundaries between styles are judgement calls. Fred Perry was worn by terrace casuals, and Dr. Martens are common across goth styles, even where the dataset files them elsewhere.
  • Matching a record isn't the same as being right. In the eval the record is the reference, and the corrections above show it had weak points.
  • Styles keep moving, so a record describes the style as researched, and it can go out of date.
