## Before: Claude from memory

**Acubi Outfit: Layered Grey Grunge (Autumn)**

Acubi is Korean-minimalist with an edge: muted greys, black and washed denim, cropped tops over long or loose bottoms, sheer layers, silver hardware and a slightly sporty-grunge feel. Here's one full look built for October weather.

The pieces

- Base layer: black sheer mesh long-sleeve. Weekday, Bershka or Monki-style mesh tops run about €15–30. This gives the outfit its signature layered look.
- Top: cropped zip-up hoodie in charcoal or heather grey. Musinsa Standard is the budget pick at about €35–45. Matin Kim is the more iconic K-brand version at about €90–120. Wear it half-zipped over the mesh.
- Bottoms: baggy washed-grey or faded black jeans, low-to-mid rise. Weekday's loose fits are about €60–70. Andersson Bell or Rest & Recreation via Musinsa or W Concept cost about €120–200. A grey pleated mini over black leggings works as an alternative.
- Shoes: slim sneakers. The Adidas Taekwondo or Puma Speedcat in black costs about €100–120. Black mesh Mary Jane flats are the softer option, about €40–80 from Zara or Korean brands on Musinsa.
- Bag: a slouchy nylon or soft-leather shoulder bag. Stand Oil costs about €70–110 and Osoi about €150–250. Both are very on-aesthetic.
- Finishing touches: chunky silver rings and a chain necklace, plus a grey beanie or ribbed leg warmers. Silver pieces are about €20–50 on Etsy or from Korean jewelry brands on W Concept. An Emis beanie is about €35, or Uniqlo's is about €15.

Where to buy

Musinsa Global and W Concept are the best sources for authentic Korean brands, and both ship to Europe. Matin Kim, Andersson Bell, Stand Oil, Emis and Musinsa Standard are all there. YesStyle is cheaper, but quality is mixed. For EU-based shopping with no import fees, Weekday, COS, Arket, Zara, Bershka and Uniqlo cover the basics well.

Budget

- Budget build: about €230–280. This uses Musinsa Standard, Weekday or Bershka, Zara flats, Etsy silver and Uniqlo accessories.
- Mid-range: about €450–550. Get the Matin Kim hoodie, Adidas Taekwondos and a Stand Oil bag, and keep Weekday jeans and high-street basics.
- Splurge: about €800–1,000. This means Andersson Bell denim, an Osoi bag, Matin Kim pieces throughout and higher-end silver.

Two notes on cost. Orders shipped from Korea to the EU include VAT and sometimes customs fees, so add roughly 20–25% unless the checkout says duties are prepaid. Prices are approximate and can shift with sales and exchange rates.

Tip: Spend money on the hoodie and the shoes, since they define the silhouette. The mesh top and accessories are where cheap options work just as well.

## After: Claude with the UnderIndex record

**Acubi Outfit: Layered Neutrals**

The palette: charcoal, washed black, grey, and one muted accent (dusty olive or faded khaki). Keep fabrics thin and slightly sheer so the layers show through each other.

- Base layer: thin asymmetrical knit top. Choose a fitted, off-shoulder or one-strap knit in grey or black. This is the easiest piece to find cheaply on YesStyle, where Korean basics-style knits typically run about $20–40.
- Over-layer: bolero. A cropped knit shrug in a contrasting neutral, such as a black bolero over a grey top, creates the signature thin-on-thin look. Hyein Seo is the brand to look at for a statement version with a darker, more deconstructed feel. Expect designer pricing, likely a few hundred dollars. Depop is your best route to a secondhand piece.
- Bottoms: distressed low-rise parachute pants. Charcoal or washed black, with drawstring hems pulled to bunch over your shoes. Thug Club fits here for an edgier, utilitarian take. Search Musinsa for Korean streetwear in this cut.
- Accent: arm warmers. These should be fine-knit and in the same tone as your bolero, so they read as part of the layering rather than as a costume. YesStyle has them for around $10–20.
- Finishing: chunky black sneakers or slim mary-janes, plus a small nylon shoulder bag. Skip logos and shine.

Budget (the model noted these are estimates it can't verify against current prices)

| Build | Approach | Total |
|---|---|---|
| Budget | YesStyle basics + Depop pants | ~$100–180 |
| Mid | One designer hero piece (Thug Club pants or a secondhand Hyein Seo bolero) + YesStyle basics | ~$250–400 |
| Full designer | New Hyein Seo + Thug Club | Likely well over $400 |

The record's $100–400 range is realistic for the first two builds. If you want to stay in that range, put your money into one piece only.

Notes on the record

- Namilia doesn't fit this look. It's a bold, provocative brand that sits much closer to flashy Cyber Y2K, which the record's own note says Acubi is not. I'd leave it out of this outfit.
- Brand availability isn't confirmed. The record doesn't say which brands are sold on which platform, so check Musinsa for Thug Club and Hyein Seo directly before assuming they're carried there.
