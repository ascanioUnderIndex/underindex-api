## Before: Claude from memory

**Goth Ninja Outfit**

Era: Goth Ninja grew out of Styleforum and Tumblr around 2011–2012 and peaked from 2012 to 2015. It was built on Rick Owens, Julius and Carol Christian Poell, with Kanye-era influence. After 2015 it split into health goth and techwear/darkwear. It's had a modest revival since about 2021 through TikTok darkwear and "archive fashion" circles.

The look: All black, with tonal greys if you want variation. The silhouette is long on top and narrow or dropped below. Layer drapey tops, use sharp leather, and keep everything matte and asymmetric.

Key pieces

- Long drape tee: Rick Owens DRKSHDW Level T or a longline tee. A cheaper option is a Uniqlo or COS longline tee, layered longer than your hoodie.
- Layer: a Julius asymmetric zip hoodie, or an Ann Demeulemeester or Rick Owens drape cardigan. A cheaper option is an asymmetric zip hoodie from ASOS or AllSaints.
- Outerwear: a Rick Owens leather biker (Stooges or Intarsia), or for a techier version, an Acronym J-series shell.
- Bottoms: Julius gas-mask cargos, Rick Owens drop-crotch sweats, or DRKSHDW Detroit jeans. A cheaper option is drop-crotch joggers from ASOS or Nike Tech Fleece in black.
- Footwear (the anchor): Rick Owens Geobaskets or Ramones, or Guidi 788Z back-zip boots. Cheaper options are DRKSHDW Ramones or Dr. Martens 1460s.
- Accessories: a large wool scarf or snood, a Rick Owens or Julius beanie, and a leather cross-body bag. Silver jewelry by Werkstatt:München suits the look, or plain silver if you're on a budget.

Where to buy

- New: SSENSE, Farfetch, Mr Porter, END., and Rick Owens' own stores and site.
- Secondhand (best value): Grailed, Vestiaire Collective, and Vinted. Grailed is the most reliable for Julius, CCP and Guidi.
- Budget basics: Uniqlo, COS, ASOS, and Zalando.

Realistic budget (approximate)

| Tier | Approach | Total |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | Fast-fashion layers + Dr. Martens | €300–500 |
| Mid | DRKSHDW pieces + secondhand Julius + used Ramones | €1,200–2,000 |
| Grail | New Rick Owens leather + Geobaskets/Guidi + Julius | €5,000–8,000+ |

## After: Claude with the UnderIndex record

**Goth Ninja Outfit (2009–2014)**

Goth Ninja is the internet-forum aesthetic of 2009 to 2014. It's built on extreme layering of thin, dark fabrics, long draped silhouettes, drop-crotch proportions and artisanal boots. It's not modern techwear, so skip the tactical straps, nylon shells and utility hardware.

The look, head to toe

- Base layer: extra-long draped t-shirt from Rick Owens (early 2010s) or 11 by BBS. Go for thin jersey in black or charcoal, long enough to fall well past the hips.
- Mid layer: hooded cardigan from Julius or Damir Doma. Wear it open over the tee so both layers hang and move.
- Bottoms: drop-crotch shorts over leggings. Get the shorts from Damir Doma or Rick Owens and the leggings from Julius or 11 by BBS. This pairing defines the Goth Ninja silhouette.
- Footwear: Rick Owens Geobaskets. These are the signature shoe.

Keep the palette to black, charcoal and washed greys. Mix textures (matte jersey, slubby knits) rather than colors.

Where to buy

- Grailed is easiest for Rick Owens, Julius and Damir Doma. Search by season to find early-2010s pieces.
- Yahoo Auctions Japan is strong for Julius and 11 by BBS, often at better prices. You'll usually need a proxy service to bid.

Realistic budget: $600–$2,500. The range is from the UnderIndex record; the per-piece split is the model's own suggested allocation, not quoted market prices.
