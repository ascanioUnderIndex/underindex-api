How this was made
  • Claude Opus 5.5 on claude.ai, a fresh incognito chat for every answer, no web search, 14 October 2026. The answers are copied verbatim.
  • From memory, the question ended with "Answer from your own knowledge; do not search the web." With the record, the record's JSON came first, then the same question.
  • Highlights are automatic: the record's key brands, and brands the dataset files under a neighbouring style. Whether a neighbouring brand is recommended or only mentioned (as history, or to rule it out) was judged by hand.
  • This is the first of two runs. Across both, answers recommending a neighbouring style's brand went from 17 of 30 to 2 of 30. All answers, the scoring scripts and the limits are in the public eval.
